The LCA-Project is a sophisticated web-based application designed for Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) analysis of building components and materials. LCA is a systematic approach to assessing the environmental impacts associated with all stages of a product's life, from raw material extraction through materials processing, manufacture, distribution, use, repair and maintenance, and disposal or recycling. This application serves as a comprehensive tool that bridges the gap between Building Information Modeling (BIM) and environmental impact assessment. The primary functionality revolves around loading Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) files, which are standardized digital descriptions of building and construction industry data, and performing detailed environmental impact calculations based on material selections and geometric properties extracted from these 3D models. The application addresses a critical need in the construction and architecture industry where professionals require accurate environmental impact assessments for sustainable.
